Semester project, Feb. – Jun. 2026 · Soft Transducers Laboratory (LMTS), EPFL Neuchâtel

Laser-induced graphene for sustainable electronics

Turning polymer and biodegradable substrates into conductive circuits with a laser, then testing whether they can sense temperature.

  • Fabricated and electrically characterized laser-induced graphene (LIG) devices on polymer and biodegradable substrates.
  • Developed LIG/Zn conductive structures on polyimide and birch wood, combining laser processing and zinc electroplating. The zinc plating strongly lowered the sheet resistance of the laser-written tracks.
  • Investigated their use as temperature sensors.

MNIS, Introduction to Finite Element Modeling, Fall 2025 · EPFL · Team of 4

Torsional NEMS device based on a graphene nanoribbon

A finite element model of a graphene ribbon suspended between two contacts, with a metal paddle at its center that rotates when a voltage is applied.

  • Modeled the coupled electrostatics and solid mechanics in COMSOL Multiphysics 6.3, with electrostatic forces from the Maxwell stress tensor.
  • Studied how voltage, paddle size, gap and ribbon width change the deflection angle and the stress. The deflection grows linearly with voltage and stress peaks at the clamped ends.
  • Computed the first torsional resonance of the device for different ribbon widths and suspended lengths.

Product Design and Systems Engineering, Fall 2025 · EPFL · Team of 5

The Guardian Angel: in-home fall prevention

A non-wearable system for elderly people living alone. Ceiling radar units detect falls and alert a wellness station that also measures heart rate, SpO2 and temperature, with a web platform for relatives and caregivers.

  • Designed and implemented the communication protocol between the ceiling units and the master station (ESP32, C++), reworked after ESP-NOW compatibility issues between ESP32 variants.
  • Integrated the sensor, communication and alert code into one working system, together with a teammate.
  • Helped select the sensors and interviewed healthcare professionals (occupational therapist, nursing home nurse, CHUV geriatrics) to define user needs.
